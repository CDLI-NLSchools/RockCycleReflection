# Lesson 3 Final Reflection

A single-file reflection activity for Grade 7 STEAM, Explore the Land, Lesson 3: The Rock Cycle. It replaces a Jamboard learning activity that no longer works.

Students begin by listing everything they know about rocks and the rock cycle, one idea per row, echoing the mind map frame on the original board. They then explain how the three rock families are connected, describe one rock journey from a starting rock type, name the most interesting thing they learned, compare their ideas with what they wrote in Lesson 1, and record one question they still have. A summary compiles their reflection with Print and Copy buttons, so students can keep it or paste it into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
